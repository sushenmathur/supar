Swipeable tile: Good Night ⇄ Festive

The first quick-action tile becomes a two-page swipe tile (drag/swipe left or right on it below). Page 1 is Good Night exactly as today. Page 2 is Festive, which toggles the festive lights/diyas. Festive page only exists until 13 Nov; after that the tile is plain Good Night again, with no dots.

Interactive: swipe the first tile, or tap Festive to switch it on/off (turns amber and glows when on).
