Dynamic lights on the Home view – interactive mock-up

Six tiles, always. Your six favourites stay put. When any other light on the Lights view turns on, it takes the place of a favourite that is off. When it goes off, the favourite comes back. Click lights on the right (or the tiles on the left) to try it.
