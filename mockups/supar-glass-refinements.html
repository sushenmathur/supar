What changes, and why

Every change keeps your layout and templates; it only adjusts values that failed contrast or looked inconsistent. Numbers match the pins on the tablet.

  1. Lit room tiles (dark) Warm gradient start 0.62 → 0.40, so white text on a lit tile goes from 3.3:1 to 5.4:1. The glow still carries the warmth. Light mode is unchanged.
  2. On toggles (dark) Theme glass-on-bg start 0.55 → 0.40, matching the lit tiles (3.5:1 → about 5:1).
  3. Night clock (light) Navy gradient was too dark on the light theme; becomes a soft blue (night-from/to), and the moon icon turns indigo so it shows (1.3:1 → 5.3:1).
  4. Day clock (dark) Sky gradient 0.55/0.45 → 0.35/0.25 so white time and city text stay readable (3.8:1 → 5.9:1).
  5. Energy colours (light) Solar, grid, home and EV hues get darker shades of the same colour in light mode; amber solar was 1.8:1 on light glass.
  6. Second lines on tinted cards Room tile labels, hot chips and clock city names use text-on-tint instead of secondary grey, which was under 3:1 on tints.
  7. Robbie (light) Teal border and icon darkened to #0f766e in light mode so the "working" state is visible.
  8. EV wattage Number stays in the main text colour; only the car icon keeps the green or teal.
  9. Greeting card Gets the soft blue greet gradient it appears to have on your tablet, instead of plain glass.
  10. Nav buttons Radius 20 → 22 to match the other small cards.
  11. Nav badge HA default red → #dc2626, so white numbers pass 4.5:1.
  12. Press and focus Every tappable card shrinks slightly (0.96) on press and shows a clear focus ring. Try tapping or tabbing in After.

When you give the go-ahead

I'll produce two ready-to-paste blocks that keep every existing property: glass.yaml with new per-mode variables (lit, on, day, night, energy hues, Robbie, greeting, badge, focus), and the button-card templates and energy card switched from hard-coded colours to those variables. You can also tell me to skip any numbered change.
