Quiet hour on the Home quick actions

Both options toggle input_boolean.quiet_hour when tapped. Tap the quiet button in either mockup to switch it on and off, or use the buttons below.

Icons: off = mdi:bell-outline, on = mdi:bell-off (silent). Colour is a calm purple so it doesn't read as an alert. Both options: tap toggles, hold opens the helper's more-info.
