Remote behind a button

The LG remote moves off the Devices page and sits behind one "TV Remote" button. The button also has power, mute and volume, so most of the time you won't need to open the remote at all. Tap the button in each phone to see how it opens.

Bottom sheet

Tap the remote button, then the dimmed area or ✕ to close

The remote slides up from the bottom and covers most of the screen, like a native iPhone sheet. You can swipe it down to close it, and the back button closes it too. Big targets, one-thumb reach.

Expand in place

Tap the remote button to open and close

Not a true pop-up. The remote unfolds under the button and pushes the page down; tap again to fold it away. It remembers open or closed across devices, because the state lives in a helper.
