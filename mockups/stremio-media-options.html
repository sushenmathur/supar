Stremio on the Media page: 3 options

Each option sits under your compact music card. Browse and Library open Bubble Card pop-ups with the full Stremio browse and library cards inside. Tap them in the mockups to preview the pop-ups. The posters are placeholders.

Cards used. A uses stremio-continue-watching-card (horizontal scroll). B uses stremio-player-card (compact) when something is playing, and a resume banner built from the continue-watching list when it isn't. C is a small launcher (button-card) with counts from sensor.…_continue_watching_count and sensor.…_library_count. All three use the same two pop-ups: #stremio-browse (browse card, 3 columns) and #stremio-library (library card with search).
