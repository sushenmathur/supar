Door status in the Security card

Option A, with the garage left out of the door count. The count covers Front, Crimsafe, Side, Laundry and Patio. When the garage is open, the heading reads "Garage open" if no doors are open, and the Garage button turns red either way.

Every state side by side

How the heading picks its words: doors open wins ("2 doors open"), then garage open ("Garage open"), otherwise "All closed". The line under it lists the open doors, and adds "Garage open" in red when the garage is open too. The pill next to "Security" only counts doors. Tapping the card still opens the security pop-up; the three buttons still work on their own.
